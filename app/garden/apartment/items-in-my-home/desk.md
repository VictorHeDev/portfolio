## Desk setup

Start with the routine, then the gear.

- What kind of work, hobbies, or rituals happen at this desk?
- What needs to be within arm’s reach? What is intentionally put away?
- What is the biggest improvement you have made to working here?
- What remains imperfect?

### [Desk item]

- What is it and what role does it play?
- Why did you choose it?
- What tradeoff did it introduce?
- Would you buy it again?
